## Offshore inquiry — only if the personal facts are correct

Use the following draft **only after confirming** that you are based in China and require employer support. It is an unsent question, not a statement of employer policy or a claim that you qualify for a visa.

> Hello [name],
>
> I'm interested in the [role title] position. I am currently based in China and would require employer support for a New Zealand work visa. Is this specific role open to offshore candidates requiring AEWV support, or does it require existing New Zealand work rights?
>
> My portfolio includes a synthetic-data document-review prototype using Next.js and FastAPI: [verified portfolio link]. I'd be happy to share a short walkthrough and discuss the parts of the implementation I have personally reviewed and worked on.
>
> Thank you, [name]

If your location or requirements are unconfirmed, replace the personal statement with:

> Could you clarify whether this role accepts offshore applicants and whether employer visa support is available for the successful candidate?

Do not claim existing work rights, relocation readiness, English proficiency, seniority or visa eligibility without verification. No employer was contacted as part of this task.
